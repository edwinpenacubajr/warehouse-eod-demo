How it works

  1. Select any role or tap Fill with sample data.
  2. Review your generated report.
  3. Tap Submit test report to simulate a successful submission.
  4. Open Supervisor summary and import it from the demo inbox.
  5. Test copy, download, print/PDF, or optional sharing.
Privacy: No external requests or messages are generated by demo submission. Drafts and test submissions stay in your browser storage. Clear demo data before using a shared device. Sharing is optional and uses your device's share feature.
